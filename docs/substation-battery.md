# Substation Battery Bank Testing

## Quarterly checks
Measure float voltage and specific gravity on every cell each quarter. Any cell more than 0.05 V below the bank average must be recorded and rechecked within 7 days.

## Charger module replacement
Replace a failed charger with part PX-4471-D. Put the bank on the portable charger before disconnecting the old module; the bank must never be left without a charger for more than 30 minutes.

## Annual load test
Perform a capacity load test once a year. Replace the bank if capacity falls below 80 percent of rated.
