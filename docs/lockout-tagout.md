# Lockout/Tagout and Arc-Flash Safety

## Lockout/tagout steps
1. Notify the control center and get a switching order number.
2. Open the isolation point and apply your personal lock and tag.
3. Test for absence of voltage with a tester you have verified on a known live source.
4. Apply protective grounds on both sides of the work area.
Only the person who applied a lock may remove it.

## Arc-flash PPE
Work on energized equipment up to 600 V requires arc-rated clothing of at least 8 cal/cm², a face shield, and voltage-rated gloves. Above 600 V, follow the task-specific arc-flash label on the equipment.

## Stop-work authority
Anyone on the crew can stop the job if conditions change or something feels unsafe. There is no penalty for using stop-work authority.
