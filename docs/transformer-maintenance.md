# Pad-Mount Transformer Maintenance

## Inspection schedule
Inspect every pad-mount transformer once every 12 months, and within 48 hours after any flood that reaches the pad. Record cabinet condition, oil level, and signs of animal intrusion in the asset app.

## Oil sampling
Take an oil sample every 24 months for dissolved gas analysis (DGA). Sample more often, every 6 months, if the last DGA showed acetylene above 2 ppm. Use a clean glass syringe and label it with the transformer ID and date.

## Bushing gasket replacement
If you see oil weeping at a bushing, replace the bushing gasket with part PX-4471-B. De-energize and ground the unit first. Torque the bushing clamp bolts to 25 ft-lb in a star pattern. Never reuse an old gasket.

## Signs to escalate
Escalate to engineering if the tank is bulging, the pressure relief device has operated, or DGA shows rising hydrogen and acetylene together.
