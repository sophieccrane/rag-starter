# Vegetation Clearance Standards

## Clearance distances
Maintain at least 10 feet of clearance around distribution primary conductors and 4 feet around secondary service drops. Plan for 3 years of regrowth when trimming.

## Hazard trees
A hazard tree is dead, leaning toward the line, or structurally damaged and tall enough to reach the conductors if it falls. Report hazard trees in the asset app with a photo.
