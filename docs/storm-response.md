# Storm Outage Response

## Restoration priorities
Restore in this order: public safety hazards (downed live wires), critical facilities (hospitals, water treatment, emergency services), substations and main feeders, then taps and individual services.

## Downed wire calls
Treat every downed wire as energized. Keep the public at least 35 feet away and stay on site until the line is confirmed de-energized or another crew relieves you.

## Crew rest
No crew member works more than 16 consecutive hours during storm response. After 16 hours, a rest period of at least 8 hours is required.
